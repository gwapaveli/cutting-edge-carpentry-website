FAQ

Common Questions

Helpful answers for homeowners planning a build, renovation, or exterior carpentry project.

Do decks in London, Ontario usually need a permit?

Deck permit requirements can depend on the municipality, deck height, guards, stairs, size, and whether the structure attaches to the home. Confirm local requirements before work starts so drawings, inspections, and timing are handled properly.

What should I send before asking for a carpentry quote?

Send the project address or nearest town, photos of the existing space, rough dimensions, drawings if you have them, timeline, budget range, and any permit or engineering details already known.

Can framing or exterior carpentry be scheduled during winter?

Some work can continue in colder months, but snow, wind, access, material handling, concrete readiness, and safety can affect the schedule. The best plan is to review the site and timing before locking in dates.

When might engineering be required for a residential project?

Engineering may be needed for load-bearing wall changes, beams, large openings, additions, roof tie-ins, covered decks, garages, shops, and other structural work. Drawings and permits should be sorted out before construction begins.
