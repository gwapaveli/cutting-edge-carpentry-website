Who we are

Family-Owned Carpentry, Led by Abe Thiessen

Cutting Edge Carpentry INC. is a family-owned carpentry and construction company based in Straffordville, Ontario, and run by owner Abram (Abe) Thiessen.

With more than 14 years of industry experience, Abe and his crew frame custom homes for homeowners and builders, and build the projects that make a home work better: decks and covered porches, fences, additions, basement framing, garages and shops, and exterior wood refinishing.

Clients deal with Abe directly, from the first call and quote through to the finished job. It's what reviewers mention most: quick answers, honest assessments, drawings that make the plan clear, and a crew that shows up on time and keeps the site clean.

  • Family-owned and operated
  • 14+ years of industry experience
  • Owner-led: you talk to Abe directly
  • Based in Straffordville, in the Municipality of Bayham
