Serving Ingersoll

Carpentry for Ingersoll's Older Homes and New Builds

Ingersoll is an Oxford County town on the Thames River, with older homes near downtown and newer neighbourhoods on its edges.

Older homes here often need structural attention before anything new goes up: a sagging porch, rotted deck framing or walls that need reframing during a renovation. Additions on these homes need careful tie-ins at the foundation and roof.

Newer homes tend to need outdoor space and finished basements: decks, fences, covered patios and basement framing. We plan these around grading, drainage and lot lines so they hold up and stay within the rules.

Ingersoll sits on Highway 401 between London and Woodstock, so it's easy to pair with other Oxford County work when we schedule.

FAQ

Common Questions About Ingersoll Carpentry Projects

Answers to the questions Ingersoll homeowners ask most before a project starts.

Do you rebuild old porches and decks in Ingersoll?

Yes. If the framing or footings are failing, a rebuild is often better value than repairs. We'll look at photos and tell you what we see.

Can you frame an addition on an older home?

Yes. Additions on older homes need a careful look at the foundation, wall framing and roofline. Drawings, and sometimes an engineer, are usually part of the process.

Who handles permits in Ingersoll?

The Town of Ingersoll's building department. We'll go over what your project needs.

Do you work in Woodstock too?

Yes. We work in Woodstock and across Oxford County, including Tillsonburg.
