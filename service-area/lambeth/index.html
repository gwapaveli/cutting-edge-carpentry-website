Serving Lambeth

Carpentry for Lambeth's Larger Lots and Newer Homes

Lambeth has been part of the City of London since 1993 but still has a village feel, with older homes on bigger lots alongside newer subdivisions.

Bigger lots open up projects that are harder to fit elsewhere in the city: detached garages and workshops, large decks with covered sections, long fence lines and additions that don't have to fight the property line.

In the newer subdivisions, requests are usually backyard upgrades: a first deck, a privacy fence, a covered patio or a finished basement. Newer homes often have straightforward framing, which helps, but grading, drainage and setback rules still need to be checked.

We also frame custom homes for builders and homeowners in and around London. If you're planning a new build in Lambeth, send the drawings and your target timeline.

Local planning notes

Planning a Project in Lambeth

What usually shapes the plan and the quote for Lambeth projects.

City of London permits

Lambeth falls under the City of London's building division. Decks, garages and additions usually need permits, and we'll confirm what's required before dates are set.

Subdivision rules

Setbacks, easements and grading plans can limit where a deck, fence or garage goes. If you have a survey or plot plan, include it with your request.

Bigger builds

Garages, workshops and additions on larger lots need planning for concrete, delivery and other trades. We coordinate the framing around the rest of the schedule.

FAQ

Common Questions About Lambeth Carpentry Projects

Answers to the questions Lambeth homeowners ask most before a project starts.

Do you build garages and workshops in Lambeth?

Yes. We frame detached garages and shops, including larger buildings. Tell us the size, door openings and what the building will be used for.

Can you build a deck and fence together?

Yes. Doing both at once lets us plan the layout, gates and stairs together. One client's Google review mentions the deck and fence we built for them in a single project.

Do I need a survey for a Lambeth project?

It isn't always required, but a survey or plot plan makes it much easier to confirm setbacks for decks, fences and garages. Include it if you have one.

Do you work in other parts of London?

Yes. We work across London, including Byron and the west end. See our London page for more.
