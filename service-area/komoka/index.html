Serving Komoka

Carpentry for Komoka's Newer Homes and Growing Neighbourhoods

Komoka and neighbouring Kilworth have grown quickly, and many homes here are new enough that owners are now adding the outdoor and basement space they didn't get from the builder.

The most common projects are backyard decks and fences, covered patios and finished basements. On newer homes these are usually straightforward, but grading, drainage swales, easements and subdivision rules still affect where things can go.

There are also larger rural lots around Komoka where detached garages, shops and additions make sense. Those builds need more planning for delivery, concrete and the other trades.

Send your address, photos and rough measurements, plus your survey if you have one, and we'll review the project.

Local planning notes

Planning a Project in Komoka

What usually shapes the plan and the quote for Komoka projects.

Middlesex Centre permits

Komoka is in the Municipality of Middlesex Centre, which issues building permits here, not the City of London. We'll confirm the requirements for your project.

Swales and easements

Many newer lots have drainage swales or easements along the back or sides. Decks and fences need to respect them, so a survey or plot plan helps.

Basements

Unfinished basements in newer homes are good candidates for framing: we lay out the rooms, frame around mechanicals and leave it ready for the next trades.

FAQ

Common Questions About Komoka Carpentry Projects

Answers to the questions Komoka homeowners ask most before a project starts.

Is Komoka under the City of London for permits?

No. Komoka is in the Municipality of Middlesex Centre, so permits go through Middlesex Centre. We'll help you understand what the application needs.

Can you build a deck on a new-build lot?

Yes. Newer lots can still be settling, so we pay attention to footings, grading and drainage. Send photos and your survey if you have one.

Do you frame basements in Komoka?

Yes. Send photos of the open space, the ceiling height and where the mechanicals are, along with a sketch of the rooms you want.

Do you work in Kilworth too?

Yes. Kilworth and the surrounding Middlesex Centre area are part of the same service area.
