Serving Simcoe

Carpentry for Simcoe's Older Homes and New Builds

Simcoe has everything from century homes near downtown to newer subdivisions on the edges of town, and each needs different carpentry.

Older homes tend to bring surprises: out-of-level floors, undersized framing, rot hidden behind siding, or a porch that has pulled away from the house. Additions and covered porches on these homes need careful tie-ins, and sometimes an engineer's input, before the new work goes up.

Newer homes are usually about adding living space: decks and covered patios off the back, privacy fences, finished basements and garages. These go faster when the yard access, grade and existing structure are clear from the start.

Simcoe is Norfolk County's main administrative centre, and building permits for the town go through the county.

  • Additions and older-home renovation framing
  • Basement framing and layout

Local planning notes

Planning a Project in Simcoe

What usually shapes the plan and the quote for Simcoe projects.

Older-home tie-ins

When new framing meets old, we check what's actually there before committing to a plan: the wall framing, the floor system, the foundation and how the new roof will connect.

Basements

Finished basements often start with framing around existing ductwork, beams and plumbing. We lay out walls to keep ceilings as high as possible and leave room for insulation.

Permits and drawings

Norfolk County handles building permits for Simcoe. Additions, raised decks and covered porches usually need drawings, and we'll tell you what to prepare.

FAQ

Common Questions About Simcoe Carpentry Projects

Answers to the questions Simcoe homeowners ask most before a project starts.

Do you work on older homes in Simcoe?

Yes. We frame additions, porches and renovation work on older homes regularly. Expect us to ask for photos of the existing structure, and to open things up carefully where the condition of the framing isn't clear.

Can you frame a basement in Simcoe?

Yes. Send photos of the open basement, the ceiling height, where the furnace, beams and plumbing are, and a sketch of the rooms you want. We frame the walls ready for electrical, insulation and drywall.

How far ahead should I book a deck or porch?

Spring and summer fill up first. If you want an outdoor project done by summer, reaching out in winter or early spring leaves time for drawings, permits and ordering materials.

Do you frame custom homes in Simcoe?

Yes. We frame custom homes for homeowners and builders across Norfolk County. Send the drawings, the lot location and your target start date and we'll review the framing scope.
