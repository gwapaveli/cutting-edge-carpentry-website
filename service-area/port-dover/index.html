Lakeside Carpentry Built for Port Dover Weather

Projects near Lake Erie take more weather than ones further inland, so how a deck, porch or cottage addition is built matters as much as how it looks.

Wind off the lake, blowing rain and freeze-thaw cycles work on every exterior connection. That's why we pay close attention to footings, fasteners and flashing where a deck or porch meets the house, and to keeping wood off the ground where it would stay wet.

A lot of Port Dover work is on seasonal or cottage properties, so timing and access matter. Let us know if the property is only used part of the year, who will be on site, and when the work needs to be finished.

We also build covered porches and porch roofs, which turn a deck into usable space on rainy or hot days. One cottage owner told us in a Google review that the new porch roof changed the entire look of the cottage.

FAQ

Common Questions About Port Dover Carpentry Projects

Answers to the questions Port Dover homeowners ask most before a project starts.

Do you build decks in Port Dover?

Yes. We build new and replacement decks, including stairs, railings and covered sections. Send photos of the area, rough measurements and how high the deck sits off the ground.

Can you add a roof over an existing deck or porch?

Often, but it depends on what's underneath. The existing posts, beams and footings have to carry the roof load, and many covered additions need a permit. We'll look at what's there before recommending anything.

Do you work on cottages that are only used in summer?

Yes. Many lakeside jobs are on seasonal properties. We'll agree on access, a schedule and how you want updates, including photos as the work goes up.

Who issues building permits in Port Dover?

Port Dover is part of Norfolk County, so permits go through the county. We'll tell you what drawings the project is likely to need.
