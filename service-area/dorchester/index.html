Serving Dorchester

Carpentry for Dorchester Homes and Thames Centre Properties

Dorchester sits just east of London in the Municipality of Thames Centre, with a mix of subdivisions, older village homes and rural properties.

That mix shows up in the work. Rural and edge-of-town properties ask for detached garages, shops and large decks. In-town homes ask for fences, covered porches, basement framing and additions.

Highway 401 makes Dorchester straightforward for the crew and material deliveries, but every site still needs a plan for where lumber lands, how the work area is protected and how the other trades fit in.

Tell us about the property and the project, and send photos, measurements and drawings so we can review it properly.

Planning a Project in Dorchester

What usually shapes the plan and the quote for Dorchester projects.

Thames Centre permits

Dorchester is in the Municipality of Thames Centre, which handles building permits here. We'll tell you what drawings and details your project likely needs.

Rural sites

Wells, septic systems and long laneways affect where a garage or shop can go and how materials get there. Mark them on a sketch or photo when you send your request.

Builder support

We frame custom homes for builders and homeowners. If you're building in Dorchester, send the plans and target start date and we'll review the framing scope.

FAQ

Common Questions About Dorchester Carpentry Projects

Answers to the questions Dorchester homeowners ask most before a project starts.

Do you build shops and garages in Dorchester?

Yes. We frame detached garages, shops and lean-tos. Include the size, door openings, ceiling height and how the building will be used.

Who issues permits in Dorchester?

The Municipality of Thames Centre. We'll go over what the application needs before work is scheduled.

Can you frame a custom home in Dorchester?

Yes. Send the drawings, lot location and your timeline. We work with homeowners and builders and coordinate with the other trades on site.

What should I send first?

The address, wide photos of the site and access, rough measurements, drawings or sketches, and when you'd like the work done.
