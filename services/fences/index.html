Quote prep

What To Send Before Requesting a Quote

For fence work, the first quote request should show layout, access, gates, grade changes, and property-line context.

  • Approximate fence length, height, style, gate count, and preferred material.
  • Photos of property lines, corners, slopes, trees, existing fences, and access for material movement.
  • Survey details or neighbour/property-line considerations if available.
  • Whether refinishing, deck work, or other exterior carpentry should be reviewed at the same time.

FAQ

Common Questions

Common questions about fences and residential carpentry support.

How long can a wood fence last in Southwestern Ontario?

Lifespan depends on materials, drainage, soil contact, sun exposure, installation details, maintenance, and weather. Regular cleaning and protection can help wood fencing hold up longer.

What should I confirm before building a fence?

Confirm property lines, gate locations, height, style, utility locates, grade changes, access, and any local fence requirements before work starts.

Can a fence be built on uneven ground?

Often, yes. Grade changes affect post layout, panel layout, gate function, and overall appearance, so the site should be reviewed before the quote is finalized.

Should exterior wood fences be refinished?

Exterior wood benefits from ongoing protection. Refinishing can improve appearance and help reduce weathering, but damaged or rotted boards may need repair or replacement first.
