FAQ

Common Questions

Common questions about custom homes and residential carpentry support.

What should be ready before custom home framing starts?

Drawings, permit status, engineering details, site access, material planning, foundation readiness, and a clear build sequence should be reviewed before framing begins.

Can you support a builder-led custom home project?

Yes. Cutting Edge Carpentry can review builder support, contractor support, and homeowner-led projects when the framing and carpentry scope is clearly defined.

How does weather affect custom home framing in Southwestern Ontario?

Wind, snow, rain, mud, and access can affect framing pace and safety. Planning the sequence, materials, and site access early helps reduce avoidable delays.

What information helps with a custom home quote?

Send the project location, drawings, structural notes, timeline, permit status, photos, and any known site constraints such as access, slope, storage, or staging limitations.
