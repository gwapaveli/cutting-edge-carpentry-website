FAQ

Common Questions

Common questions about home renovations and residential carpentry support.

When should structural renovation work be reviewed by an engineer?

Engineering may be needed for load-bearing wall changes, beams, large openings, roof or floor changes, and major tie-ins. Confirm the drawing, permit, and engineering path before work starts.

What carpentry work is common during a renovation?

Renovation carpentry can include new wall framing, openings, blocking, structural tie-ins, exterior woodwork, basement framing, stairs, and coordination points for the next trades.

What should I send for a renovation quote?

Send photos of the existing space, rough measurements, project goals, property location, drawings if available, timeline, and any known permit or engineering information.

Can you work around other renovation trades?

Yes, but the carpentry scope and sequence should be clear. Knowing when electrical, plumbing, HVAC, insulation, and drywall are expected helps the framing handoff stay cleaner.
