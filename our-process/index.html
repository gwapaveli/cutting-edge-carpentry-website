Our Carpentry Process

Project flow

From Request to Handoff

The process is built around clear project details, practical planning, and straightforward communication before work begins.

  1. 01

    Request

    Send the project type, location, photos, timing, drawings, measurements, and any permit or engineering details you already have.

  2. 02

    Review

    The team reviews scope, fit, access, materials, sequencing, and what is needed before a quote or site visit.

  3. 03

    Quote planning

    Details are clarified so pricing and schedule expectations are based on the actual job, not guesswork.

  4. 04

    Scheduling

    Approved work is scheduled around site readiness, weather, material availability, and other trades where needed.

  5. 05

    Build

    The carpentry work is completed with practical coordination and updates around the work being done.

  6. 06

    Handoff

    The project is left ready for the next stage, next trade, inspection, or everyday use depending on the scope.

Project organization

Precision Planning, No Guesswork

At Cutting Edge Carpentry, exceptional results require absolute precision. After you submit a project request, our team reaches out to schedule a consultation and discuss your vision in detail.

Once the requirements are clear, we use TradeWorks Hub, a construction management platform built by contractors, for contractors, to connect scheduling, project management, and time tracking in one streamlined system.

For clients, that workflow means accurate data, transparent communication, and a more efficient building experience from the initial digital design to the final invoice.

  • Exact material takeoffs help create highly accurate quotes with fewer invoice surprises.
  • Built-in trade calculators support precise, code-compliant planning.
  • Detailed drawings can help smaller decks, fences, and pergolas move through permits faster.
