FAQ

Common Questions

Helpful details for sending a clear project request.

What photos help when requesting a quote?

Wide photos of the area, close-ups of problem spots, access points, existing structure, grade changes, and any drawings or sketches help the team understand the project before a call or site visit.

Should I include drawings, permits, or engineering notes?

Yes. For framing, additions, garages, shops, covered decks, structural openings, or custom home work, drawings and engineering notes can change the quote, schedule, and build approach.

How early should I reach out before a build?

Reach out as early as possible for custom homes, additions, garages, shops, basement framing, decks, or projects that may need permits, engineering, materials planning, or coordination with other trades.

What locations should I mention in the request?

Include the exact address or nearest town, especially if the project is outside London or Straffordville. Location affects travel, scheduling, material planning, and whether the project is a good fit.
